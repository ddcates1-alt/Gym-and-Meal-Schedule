Constants & Rules — Both Lifters

Cates calories
Training days 2,250 · rest days 2,100 — never eat back exercise calories. Cuts come from carbs and fat only; protein stays pinned.
Ruben calories
2,500–2,800 to gain — same food, bigger plate, extras his
Cates protein
150–170 g across 3 feedings (~40–60 g each)
Creatine
5 g monohydrate every day — both lifters. Mr. Cates: noon shake. Ruben: any daily drink. Ruben: expect 2–4 lbs water weight gain over first 3–4 weeks.
Water
70–90 oz (per labs)
Effort — Cates
Every set stops 1–2 reps shy of failure; goal is holding or nudging weights up as fat comes off
Effort — Ruben
Progressive overload — a little weight or a rep or two each week
Progression
3+ reps left in tank on final set of 10–12 → barbells +5 lbs, dumbbells one increment, machines one plate
Tendon guardrail
Ruben's squat and deadlift capped at +5 lbs per week once at working weight, no matter how easy it feels
Barbell rule
Barbells are Ruben's, dumbbells and machine pins are Mr. Cates' — no plate-swapping between sets
Spotting
During Ruben's bench, squat, and deadlift: Mr. Cates spots, never lifts. Weight confirmed aloud before every barbell set. Safety bars always set before squats.
Hinges — Cates
Mid-shin hard stop on RDL — no end range under load (hypermobility rule)
Back rule
Any rep that feels wrong in the back ends the set — both lifters
Machine pins
Numbers assume pound-labeled stacks; if unlabeled or kg, find hard-but-clean at target reps and log it as baseline
Strength tripwire
Final-set reps dropping two Sunday reviews running → rest days return to 2,200
Sunday review
Weight trend (ignore daily noise until week 3), waist at navel, calibration numbers for both lifters
