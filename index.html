Table 3 — Constants & Rules

Every day
Calories
2,200–2,300 fixed — never eat back exercise calories
Protein
150–170 g across 3 feedings (~40–60 g each)
Creatine
5 g monohydrate in the noon shake, every day
Water
70–90 oz (per labs)
Fish oil / D3
1–2 g EPA/DHA daily · D3 dose pending bloodwork
Effort
Every set stops 1–2 reps shy of failure
Progression
3+ reps left in tank on final set → up one increment next time
Hinges
Mid-range loading only — no end range under load
Sunday review
Weight trend (ignore daily noise until week 3), waist at navel, log calibration numbers for you + Ruben
Protein short?
Full Creami pint instead of half (+30 g) or swap snack to cottage cheese
Shared meals
Same food, different plates — Ruben gets the extras, yours get weighed
