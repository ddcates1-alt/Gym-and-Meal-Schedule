Monday — Review + Solo

Today
Weekly Review — first thingWeight trend (ignore daily noise until week 3), waist at navel with soft tape, calibration numbers for both lifters. Full week through Sunday now logged. 3+ reps in the tank on any final set → up one increment this week: barbells +2.5 lbs per side, dumbbells one increment, machines one plate. Squat and deadlift never more than +2.5 per side per week. Two consecutive Mondays with dropping reps → rest-day calories return to 2,200.
CardioElliptical 25 min, easy pace.
Solo work — Mr. Cates only
Dumbbell Bench Press
MR. CATES — SOLO3 × 10–1235 lb per hand · lighter than Tuesday by design
Lie flat, dumbbells at chest level, press to arms extended. Lighter than your Tuesday session — this is supplementary volume, not a second max effort day. Do this before the elliptical so strength isn't blunted.
Dumbbell Skull Crusher
MR. CATES — SOLO3 × 1235 lb dumbbell, both hands · after bench
Lying on the bench, one dumbbell held vertically in both hands, arms extended over your chest. Lower it behind your head by bending only at the elbows, then extend back up. Upper arms stay fixed and vertical the whole rep — if the elbows drift back toward your head, the shoulders are taking over. Control the lowering; this is the exercise where momentum causes problems.
Cable Crossover
MR. CATES — SOLO3 × 10–12pin 8 · warm-up weight, squeeze focus
High pulleys, one handle per hand, step forward. Arc down and together until hands meet at hip height, hold the squeeze a beat, control the return. Pin 8 is deliberately light — this is quality work, not load.

Tuesday — Push

Today
With Ruben · ~75 min · 90 sec rest · 2–3 min after barbell work · plates change between sets
Barbell Bench Press
RUBEN3 × 10–1220 lbs per side start · working 25–35 per side
MR. CATES3 × 10–1235 lbs per side start · adjust to hard-but-clean at 12
Same bar, alternating sets — one lifts, the other spots and changes plates. Stand at the head of the bench, hands hovering under the bar, guide the unrack and re-rack. Confirm the weight aloud before every set — Ruben can't plate-check visually, and the plates now change between every set. All numbers are plates per side; the bar is not counted. Mr. Cates: 35 per side is a starting estimate off your 40 lb dumbbells — calibrate on the first set and log the real number.
Incline Barbell Press
RUBEN3 × 10–1215 lbs per side start · working 20–30 per side
MR. CATES3 × 10–1225 lbs per side start · adjust to hard-but-clean at 12
Bench set to about 30 degrees — shifts work to the upper chest. Same bar, alternating sets, plates change between turns. Incline always runs lighter than flat bench, roughly 70–75% — that's normal, not a step backward. Spot from the head of the bench and confirm the weight aloud every set. Plates per side, bar not counted.
Seated DB Shoulder Press
RUBEN3 × 10–1235 lb per hand
MR. CATES3 × 10–1230 lb per hand · ribs down, no arch
Seated upright with back support. Mr. Cates: your dancer mobility will let the spine arch to finish a rep — that's flexibility doing the shoulders' job. Keep the ribcage stacked over the pelvis.
Lateral Raises
RUBEN3 × 10–1210 lb · light by design
MR. CATES3 × 10–1210 lb · can run simultaneous
Arms at your sides, raise out to shoulder height, lower with control. Light on purpose — the side delt is a small muscle and heaving turns this into a shrug. This weight may never change, and that's correct.
Dips
RUBEN — REQUIRED3 × 10–12assisted machine or parallel bars · this is his triceps work
MR. CATES3 × 10–12dips or push-ups · optional finisher
Ruben: no longer optional. With the pushdown removed, dips are the only direct triceps work in his week — everything else is compound pressing. Use the assisted machine if bodyweight is too much; assistance is fine, skipping isn't. Lower until the upper arms are about parallel to the floor, chest leading slightly forward and elbows tracking back. Don't sink deeper than that — the bottom of a dip is where shoulders get hurt. Mr. Cates: yours stays optional; you have skull crushers four days a week.
Cable Crossover
RUBEN3 × 10–12pin 20 each side
MR. CATES3 × 10–12pin 20 each side · squeeze at centre
Arc down and together until hands meet at hip height. The squeeze at the bottom is the rep — hold a beat before the return. If you can't feel the chest working, the weight is too high; drop the pin.
Rear Delt Cable Fly
RUBEN3 × 10–12pin 10 each side
MR. CATES3 × 10–12pin 10 each side · keep it light
Same station as crossover — turn to face the stack, handles stay put. Pull out and back at shoulder height, elbows leading, not hands pulling sideways. Think: show your elbows to the wall behind you. This is the antidote to all your pressing volume — never load it heavily.

Thursday — Pull

Today
With Ruben · ~75 min · 90 sec rest · 2–3 min after deadlifts
Deadlift
RUBEN3 × 5–645 lbs per side start (one plate) · working 55–70 per side · +2.5 per side/wk cap
MR. CATES — DUTYEvery setconfirm weight aloud · watch his back · no lifting
Bar over mid-foot, hips push back, chest up, back flat. Push the floor away. Deadlifts can't be hand-spotted — a failing rep gets set down. Your intervention is your voice. Watch from the side for the lower back rounding, especially on the last rep. Any rep that rounds ends the set.
Pull-ups / Lat Pulldown
RUBEN3 × 10–12pull-ups first at 129 lbs BW · 10 clean = skip pulldown
MR. CATES3 × 10–12pulldown pin 95 · adjust to clean 12-rep weight
Same movement, two directions. Start each rep by pulling the shoulder blades down before the arms bend. Think "elbows down and back," not "hands to chin." Full stretch at the top, no bounce out of the bottom.
Seated Row Machine
RUBEN3 × 10–12calibrate to hard-but-clean · log your number
MR. CATES3 × 10–12calibrate to hard-but-clean · log your number
Chest against the pad, fixed handles — Ruben reaches the same place every set, and the seat sets his position for him. Pull the handles back to your ribs, elbows tracking close to the body, squeeze the shoulder blades at the end, control the return. Chest stays on the pad. If the machine has independent arms, one side at a time also works and covers unilateral pulling.
Face Pulls
RUBEN3 × 10–12pin 30
MR. CATES3 × 10–12pin 30 · keep light — shoulder health
High pulley, rope, palms facing each other. Pull toward the bridge of your nose, hands separating as they come, finishing with knuckles beside your ears and elbows high. Never chase weight here — heavy turns it into a row and bypasses the rotator cuff entirely.
Curls
RUBEN3 × 10–12 barbellEZ bar + 10 lbs per side · then hammer 3 × 10–12 @ 25 lb
MR. CATESHammer 3 × 10–1225 lb · then stretch while Ruben finishes
Hammer grip means palms facing each other throughout — like holding two hammers. Elbows stay at your sides; no swinging the torso to start the rep.
Core — Plank
RUBEN3 × 30–45 secforearms down · no equipment, no navigation
MR. CATES3 × 30–45 secpush the floor away · don't let the chest sink
Forearms down, elbows under the shoulders, one straight line from head to heels. Squeeze the glutes — that's what stops the hips sagging. Mr. Cates: hypermobility shows up here as the shoulder blades sinking so the chest drops between them. Actively push the floor away to keep the upper back supported rather than collapsed.
Core — Pilates 100s
RUBEN100 pumpsfloor-based · cue the count and rhythm aloud
MR. CATES100 pumpslegs 45° or higher — non-negotiable
On your back, head and shoulders curled off the mat, legs extended, arms straight at your sides pumping small and fast — five pumps in, five pumps out, ten breath cycles. Mr. Cates: legs at 45° or higher. Lower legs mean more lumbar extension, and your hypermobility will let you sink into a range you shouldn't hold. If the lower back lifts off the mat, raise the legs until it flattens — same principle as the mid-shin rule. Ruben: he can't watch the arm pump, so cue the rhythm and count aloud the first few sessions.

Saturday — Legs

Today
With Ruben · ~75 min · 90 sec rest · 2–3 min after squats
Smith Machine Squat
RUBEN3 × 10–1225 lbs per side start · working 35–50 per side · +2.5 per side/wk cap
MR. CATES — DUTYSet catchesconfirm height aloud before every set
Feet slide 6–8 inches forward of where they'd be under a free barbell — the fixed vertical bar path requires this to keep shin angle right and protect the knees. The safety catches are Ruben's spotter: set them just below his bottom position, and confirm the height with him aloud before every set. The guided bar means he can find it consistently every time.
Romanian Deadlift
RUBEN3 × 10–12barbell, 20 lbs per side
MR. CATES3 × 10–1235 lb per hand · HARD STOP mid-shin
Soft knee bend locked in place — the knee angle doesn't change. Push the hips back, let the weight drag down your legs. Mr. Cates: mid-shin is the floor. Your hamstrings will want more depth because the stretch feels productive — it isn't under load, and the connective tissue hasn't earned it. If the dumbbells pass your ankles, the set is already wrong.
Leg Press
RUBEN3 × 10–12pin 95 · calibration
MR. CATES3 × 10–12pin 115 · shared machine, move pin
Feet flat, shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Lower until the knees reach about 90 degrees — never let the lower back peel off the seat. Drive through the whole foot, not the toes. Don't lock the knees at the top. Both pins are calibration guesses; adjust same-day if 12 reps feels empty.
Leg Curls
RUBEN3 × 10–12pin 45 · calibration
MR. CATES3 × 10–12pin 55
Face down, pad on the back of the ankles, curl the heels toward your glutes. Hips stay pressed into the pad the whole rep. Letting the hips rise means the lower back is helping instead of the hamstrings — if that happens, the weight is too high.
Goblet Squat
RUBEN3 × 10–1230 lb dumbbell at chest
MR. CATES3 × 10–1235 lb dumbbell at chest
Hold one dumbbell vertically at chest height, both hands cupped under the top end. Feet shoulder-width, toes angled out slightly. Squat down between your knees — the weight out front automatically keeps the torso upright, no thinking required. Stable and safe for both lifters, nothing to navigate.
Calf Raises
RUBEN3 × 10–12pin 65 or 25 lb DBs
MR. CATES3 × 10–1230 lb DBs · optional
Heels hanging off a step gives the best range. Rise onto the balls of the feet as high as possible, pause at the top for a beat, lower all the way down with control. Calves are springy and will bounce through reps if you let them — slow and full-range is the dose.

Constants & Rules — Both Lifters

Rep standard
3 × 10–12 on everything — the one exception is Ruben's deadlift at 3 × 5–6, which never changes
Ruben's triceps
Dips are required, not optional. The pushdown was dropped as redundant, so dips are his only direct triceps work. Assisted machine counts; skipping does not.
Rest periods
90 seconds between sets · 2–3 minutes after bench, deadlift, and squat
Creatine
5 g monohydrate daily — both lifters. Ruben: expect 2–4 lbs water weight over the first 3–4 weeks. That's muscle fullness, not fat.
Effort — Cates
Every set stops 1–2 reps shy of failure. The goal is holding or nudging weights up as fat comes off.
Effort — Ruben
Progressive overload — a little weight or a rep or two each week
Progression
3+ reps left in the tank on the final set → barbells +2.5 lbs per side, dumbbells one increment, machines one plate
Tendon guardrail
Ruben's squat and deadlift capped at +2.5 lbs per side per week (5 lbs total) once at working weight, no matter how easy it feels
Barbell loading
All barbell numbers in this program are plates PER SIDE, not total. The bar is not counted. A standard Olympic bar is 45 lbs; EZ bars run 15–25 lbs; Smith machine bars vary by machine and some are counterbalanced. Load what the page says on each side and ignore the bar.
Barbell rule
Barbells are Ruben's; dumbbells and machine pins are Mr. Cates'. No plate-swapping between sets.
Spotting
On Ruben's bench, deadlift, and Smith squat: Mr. Cates spots or supervises, never lifts. Weight confirmed aloud before every barbell set. Safety catches set and confirmed before every squat set.
Hinges — Cates
Mid-shin hard stop on RDL — no end range under load. Non-negotiable hypermobility rule.
Back rule
Any rep that feels wrong in the back ends the set — both lifters, no exceptions
Machine pins
Numbers assume pound-labeled stacks. If unlabeled or in kg, find hard-but-clean at target reps and log that as baseline.
Monday review
Weight trend (ignore daily noise until week 3), waist at navel with soft tape, calibration numbers for both lifters — the full week through Sunday is logged by then
Strength tripwire
Final-set reps dropping two Monday reviews running → rest-day calories return to 2,200
Solo days
Mon / Wed / Fri / Sun — bench 35s, skull crushers 35 lb, crossover pin 8, then elliptical 25 min. Lift before cardio so strength isn't blunted.
